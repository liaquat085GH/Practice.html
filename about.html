About Horizon Institute

Fifteen years of shaping skilled professionals and confident graduates.

Who We Are

A Campus Built Around Student Success

Founded in 2011, Horizon Institute of Technology began with a single computer lab and 60 students. Today we are a full campus offering professional IT courses, Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE) programs and formal intermediate education (FSc, ICS and I.Com).

Our approach is simple: strong fundamentals, practical training and personal mentoring, so every learner is ready for university or the job market.

  • Registered with the provincial technical education board
  • Affiliated with the local intermediate board
  • Student-teacher ratio of 20:1
